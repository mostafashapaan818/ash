01 / PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY & ABOUT

Practical architectural engineering from shop drawings to handover.

Ahmed Mohamed Mahmoud Shaaban is an Architectural Engineer and Finishing Department Zone Manager at EDARA SODIC who delivers residential, retail, hospitality, and corporate interiors from technical drawings through final handover.

Since starting as a Site Engineer in 2014, he has managed interior finishing across East Cairo zones (Villette, Eastown, SODIC East, and EDNC) and executed over 25 commercial, F&B, banking, and villa projects on schedule.
